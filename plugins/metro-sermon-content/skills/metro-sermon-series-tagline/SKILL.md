# Metro sermon series tagline

A series tagline is one sentence that tells a first-time visitor what the series is and why it matters. It points to Jesus.

## Pattern

Start with "Join us each Sunday as we". Name what the church does in the series, then what God does, then how that shapes the listener. Use plain words. Write about God and his people, not about Metro.

Reference examples:

"Join us each Sunday as we walk through Exodus and see how God rescues his people and points us to Jesus."

"Join us each Sunday as we see how God gave his Son for us and how his generosity makes us a people who give freely."

For a book series, name the book. For a topical series, name the theme in the sentence.

## Inputs

You need the series name and either the book or the theme. If neither is known, ask once. Do not invent a theme. If the series covers passages the user can share, read them and keep the sentence true to the text.

## Output

Give two or three options of one sentence each. Mark which one is closest to the reference examples.

If the user wants a card subtitle, write one short line in the same tone. It carries one image or promise. Do not repeat the tagline. Check it against any tagline already on the card.

## House rules

No em dashes. Straight quotes. Present tense. Warm, full sentences. No churchy jargon. Use the metro-copy-voice skill when it is installed.
